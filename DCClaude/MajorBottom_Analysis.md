### Note on BB<Lower: It's a Flash Signal

BB<Lower spikes on crash days and resets within 1-2 days. The COVID crash demonstrates this clearly:
- **16 Mar 2020**: BB<Lower = 44% (crash day)
- **31 Mar 2020**: BB<Lower = 9% (15 days later — band expanded, stocks recovered above)

Same pattern in current correction:
- **23 Mar 2026**: BB<Lower = 12%
- **27 Mar 2026**: BB<Lower = 1% (4 days later)

The more reliable slow-moving signals are URSI (stays oversold for days), DD>=15% (structural), PE percentile (cycle-adjusted), and PB<1 (deep value).

## Key Patterns (Updated with Apr 2025)

### Pattern 1: Brokerage Leads Most Bounces — But Not All
Brokerage is the #1 sector in 2W bounces (22% of slots 2022-2025). **SHS** appears in top quartile at 4/4 recent bottoms. **BSI** is reliable for 2W (3/4). However, in macro/tariff-driven bottoms (Apr 2025), Chemicals and industrials can take leadership instead.

### Pattern 2: "Most Beaten = Most Bounce" in Deep Corrections
Strong negative correlation (-0.46 to -0.68) in deep corrections. In Nov 2022, oversold stocks returned +34.2% vs +17.1% for the rest. This effect is weak in mild corrections.

### Pattern 3: Chemicals Are the Most Consistent Bouncers
DDV and DCM appear across all correction types — financial (2022) and macro (2025). **DDV** is the single best 2W performer: +53.2% (Jan-21), +22.0% (Jul-21), +46.9% (Apr-25).

### Pattern 4: Banking Lags — Pick Mid-Tier If You Must
Banking underperforms market median in 3 of 4 recent bottoms. Large-cap banks (VCB, VPB) are the worst bouncers. If playing banks, choose **SHB, STB, EIB** over the big names.

### Pattern 5: Context Determines Leadership
- **Financial/liquidity crisis** (2022): Brokerage + Residential lead
- **Macro/tariff shock** (2025): Chemicals + Metals + Industrials lead
- **Always present**: HAG, SGP, DCM, DDV — structural bouncers regardless of context
